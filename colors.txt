Core palette (locked, from notes.txt)
  Deep Mocha    #433633  dark brown/olive - primary text + dark accents
  Cool Steel    #A4A9AD  neutral grey     - borders, muted fills, neutral tags
  Stormy Teal   #00635D  primary accent   - buttons, links, seats-ok, focus rings
  Pumpkin Spice #EE7B30  warm accent      - warnings, conflicts, term badge
  Powder Blue   #9EB3C2  light tint       - background washes, hover states, grid lines

Supplementary colors (new)
  Lecture Blue  #46688A  lecture blocks/tags (LE)
  Discussion Green #4E7239  discussion blocks/tags (DI)
  Lab Purple    #6E5B8E  lab blocks/tags (LAB)

Rationale
The five core colors are enough for chrome (backgrounds, buttons, warnings),
but lecture/discussion/lab blocks need to be distinguishable at a glance on
the calendar. Using teal for lectures and pumpkin for discussions caused two
problems: discussions were indistinguishable from conflict warnings, and the
calendar lost its categorical coding entirely.

The three supplementary colors are:
- Deliberately desaturated and mid-dark (~35-45% lightness) so white text
  passes contrast on them and they sit at the same visual weight as the core
  palette.
- Hue-shifted versions of what they replace: Lecture Blue is Powder Blue
  (#9EB3C2) pushed darker/bluer, keeping the blue family from the original
  UCSD scheme. Discussion Green and Lab Purple keep the original semantic
  hues (green for DI, purple for labs) but are toned down to match the muted,
  earthy character of the new palette.
- Distinct from Stormy Teal (blue-ish green) and Cool Steel (pure grey), so
  all six block categories (LE/DI/LAB/other/conflict/finals-tint) read
  uniquely on the grid.

No other colors were added; everything else in the UI derives from the five
core colors via opacity/tinting (e.g. rgba washes of powder/pumpkin/mocha).
